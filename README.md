# My Favorite Movies
1. [Ready Player One](https://hdtodayz.to/watch-movie/watch-ready-player-one-hd-19742.5297335)
   
      Ready Player One is a science fiction adventure film set in the year 2045, where most of humanity escapes their harsh reality by entering a vast virtual world called the OASIS. The story follows a young gamer named Wade Watts, who joins a global competition to find a hidden Easter egg left by the game’s late creator. Whoever finds the egg will inherit the creator’s fortune and full control of the OASIS. Along the way, Wade faces powerful enemies and forms alliances with other players who share his dream of freedom and fairness. The movie highlights the importance of friendship, courage, and staying connected to the real world despite the lure of virtual escape.

2. [How to Train Your Dragon item](https://hdtodayz.to/watch-movie/watch-how-to-train-your-dragon-hd-19703.5349049)


      How to Train Your Dragon is an animated adventure film that tells the story of a young Viking named Hiccup who lives in a village where dragons are feared and hunted. Unlike the other Vikings, Hiccup is curious and kind-hearted, and his life changes when he befriends an injured dragon he names Toothless. Through their friendship, Hiccup learns that dragons are not the dangerous creatures his people believe them to be. Together, they show the village that humans and dragons can live in peace and work as one. The movie teaches lessons about courage, understanding, and accepting differences
